Benchmark Narrative Walkthrough

  1. Purchase: 60 kg flour bought at ₹45/kg (closing balance 100 + 60 - 40 = 120 kg).
  2. Production Run: Murukku 200g, 1 batch. Standard oil is 10 L; supervisor issued 10.5 L (+5% variance flagged).
  3. Output Recording: 188 good packets (planned 200, yield variance -6% flagged), 6 pouches damaged, 9 cartons used (closing pouches 306).
  4. Salesman A (Mobile): Orders 150 packets for Retailer A (Tamil Nadu intra-state).
  5. Admin Verification: Verifies stock (238 available > 150), approves, generates GST invoice (₹7,500 taxable + ₹187.50 CGST + ₹187.50 SGST = ₹7,875), and dispatches (closing stock 88 pkts).
  6. Salesman B (Out-of-State): Orders 120 packets for Distributor B (Karnataka). System detects shortage of 32 packets, blocks dispatch, and computes IGST ₹300.00.
  7. Payment Collection: Records ₹4,000 payment from Retailer A; outstanding balance becomes exactly ₹3,875.00.
  8. Costing: Overheads allocated (Labour ₹1,200 + Power ₹600) -> ₹31.14 per packet.
